Playground

Type Snowflake SQL. SQLQuack parses it, checks it, translates it to DuckDB SQL and runs it in DuckDB, all in your browser. Each stage passes or fails on its own, so you can see which step stopped and where.

Hover an underline for its diagnostic.

Fixture rows for DuckDB

A policy file, as for --policy.
